    Reading the routing record

    What collectors saw

    RIPEstat groups observed announcements by prefix and origin ASN. Visibility is the share of RIS full-feed peers that saw the origin at that time. Overlapping origins can appear in the same period.

    What a gap means

    A gap can reflect collector coverage or the minimum peer setting. Use traceroutes for packet geography and treat route visibility as a sample of the global BGP system.

    Follow the source

    RIPE RIS publishes Routing History, BGP State, and Announced Prefixes. RPKI Guard checks whether the origin and prefix pair is authorized today. Use is subject to the RIPEstat terms.
